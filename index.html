About this Service

This portal allows citizens to confidentially report cases of harassment, family distress, workplace issues, or threats. All submissions are stored securely and reviewed by authorized officers. A unique Case ID is generated for tracking the status of every complaint.

For emergencies requiring immediate assistance, please contact your local emergency number directly. This system does not automatically contact the police.
